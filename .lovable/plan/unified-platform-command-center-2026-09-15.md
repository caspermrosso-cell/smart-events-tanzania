# Unified Platform Command Center

## Goal
Refresh the signed-in platform using the selected Sand & Forest command-center direction, while preserving all existing workflows and permissions.

## What will change
- Reorganize the main navigation into clear work areas: Overview, Events, Communications, Guest Experience, Finance & Documents, Insights, and Administration.
- Add a Communications workspace that clubs SMS and WhatsApp as sibling channels with clear switching and quick access to their tools.
- Rebuild the signed-in home dashboard as a compact guided playbook with grouped workspace cards, live summary metrics, and direct actions.
- Refine SMS Management with a clearer header, allocation overview, icon-led navigation, and stronger compose/recipient hierarchy.
- Refine WhatsApp Management with matching channel navigation, clearer reporting hierarchy, and more legible delivery/response states.
- Apply the locked Sand & Forest palette and confident geometric typography to signed-in screens, with restrained motion and mobile-friendly layouts.

## Technical details
- Keep existing routes, permissions, queries, sending flows, templates, reports, logs, and invoicing behavior unchanged.
- Introduce shared workspace/navigation presentation components rather than duplicating channel UI.
- Use semantic theme tokens and existing interface controls; use Lucide icons only.
- Verify signed-in dashboard, SMS, WhatsApp, and mobile navigation in the running preview.
